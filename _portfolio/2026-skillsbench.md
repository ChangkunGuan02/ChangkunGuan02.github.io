Large language model agents are increasingly equipped with **skills**: structured packages of procedural knowledge that tell an agent how to carry out a particular kind of task. SkillsBench measures how much these skills actually help.

The benchmark spans 87 tasks across 8 domains and evaluates when, and by how much, curated skills improve agent performance on diverse real-world tasks. It is a large community effort, and I contributed as one of its co-authors.

[Paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12670)
