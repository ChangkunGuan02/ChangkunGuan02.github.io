SkillsBench is a large community benchmark measuring how structured procedural knowledge packages ("skills") improve the performance of large language model agents. The benchmark spans 87 tasks across 8 domains, and evaluates when and how much curated skills help agents complete diverse real-world tasks.

I contributed as one of the benchmark's many co-authors.
