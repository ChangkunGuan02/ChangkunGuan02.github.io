Real-time order fulfillment requires solving sequential contextual stochastic programs under tight latency constraints. This paper — the extended version of our [NeurIPS 2025 ML&times;OR workshop paper](/publication/2025-order-fulfillment/) — develops neural optimization proxies that learn to approximate the solutions of these programs for real-time deployment.

The learned proxies make decisions roughly 2,800&times; faster than traditional optimization solvers while delivering superior fulfillment cost outcomes and reducing late deliveries by about half compared to established fulfillment policies.
